Games

Games to play in your browser: a platform game through Europe and escape rooms full of puzzles. Free, without ads, on a computer, a phone or a tablet. Every game is a web app you can install; TripScouts is also an Android app. The code of every game is open source.

TripScouts

Platform game · for the whole family

Two families want to go on holiday together, but they can't agree where. So you go ahead as their trip scout: ten trips on the map of Europe, from the snowy Ardennes and the North Pole to summer in the Ardèche and the Veneto, with a new level every day of the week. Walk, jump, swim and crack code locks, then give your verdict.
